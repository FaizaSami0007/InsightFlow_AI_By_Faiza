# Explainability & Evidence UX

## Response anatomy

1. Direct answer
2. Key evidence
3. Method used
4. Relevant filters/time range
5. Limitations or uncertainty
6. Optional "show calculation" affordance

## Example

> North generated the highest revenue.

Evidence:
- Metric: revenue (sum)
- Dimension: region
- Dataset version: v7
- Rows considered: 48,213
- Highest value: $1.82M

The user can expand the evidence panel without leaving the conversation.

## Avoid

- unexplained confidence percentages
- fake citations
- claims of causality from correlation
- excessive chain-of-thought exposure

Expose concise, verifiable evidence rather than private reasoning traces.
