# Enterprise KPI & Revenue Performance Policy 2026

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
This policy defines the core key performance indicators (KPIs), operational margins, anomaly thresholds, and scenario forecasting parameters for InsightFlow AI enterprise deployments.

## 2. Target Revenue and Margin Benchmarks
- **Target Monthly Revenue Growth**: 15.0% quarter-over-quarter.
- **Enterprise AI Product Line**: Minimum expected Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) is **4.75 / 5.0**. Target deal size exceeds $100,000.00.
- **Automated BI Product Line**: High-volume low-touch tier. Minimum conversion rate threshold is **7.0%**.
- **Cloud Analytics Product Line**: Retention rate benchmark is **92.0%**, with direct sales conversion target of **9.0%**.

## 3. Marketing ROI & Spend Guardrails
- **Digital Ad Spend Efficiency**: Target Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) is under $450.00.
- **Partnership Revenue Contribution**: Should represent at least 25% of European quarterly sales.
- **Marketing Spend Allocation**: Digital Ads (40%), Direct Sales & Events (45%), Partnerships & Email (15%).

## 4. Anomaly Detection Thresholds
- Any sales day dipping more than **2.5 standard deviations (Z-score > 2.5)** from the 30-day moving average must trigger an automated P1 Anomaly Alert.
- Customer satisfaction drops below **4.2 / 5.0** in any region require root-cause attribution and automated mitigation task dispatch.

## 5. What-If Scenario Assumptions
- **Bull Case**: +20% Marketing Spend increase yields +15% Lead Generation with constant conversion rate.
- **Base Case**: +10% Marketing Spend increase yields +8% Lead Generation.
- **Stress Test**: -15% Revenue shock with +10% inflation in acquisition costs.
